HTML ELEMENTS

Most HTML elements have an opening tag, content, and a closing tag, for example <p>This is text.</p>. Void elements have no content and no closing tag; examples include <br>, <img>, <hr>, and <input>.

Elements can be nested, but they must close in the reverse order in which they opened. Attributes belong in the opening tag and provide extra information, such as an image's src or a link's href.

I practised headings, lists, forms, tables, semantic elements, and text formatting. Choose an element for its meaning, and use CSS when the goal is visual styling.
